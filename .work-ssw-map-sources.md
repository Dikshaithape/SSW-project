# SSW map source notes

The updated public page uses city latitude/longitude values in decimal degrees and links each city marker to a Google Maps query URL. Google Maps documents decimal-degree coordinate entry and right-click coordinate retrieval here:

- https://support.google.com/maps/answer/18539?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop

Public SSW context is provisional until the user's research paper is supplied. NOAA's SSW Compendium describes major SSW events as rapid midwinter stratospheric warming events associated with polar-vortex wind reversal and notes possible winter surface impacts including increased cold-air outbreaks over North America and Eurasia:

- https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/22108
- https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-9-63-2017
