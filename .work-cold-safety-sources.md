# Cold-safety source notes

The public-awareness precaution levels use practical guidance adapted from official sources:

- Ready.gov Winter Weather: prepare home supplies, vehicle kits, official alerts; avoid roads when possible; use generators outdoors; watch for frostbite and hypothermia.
  https://www.ready.gov/winter-weather
- National Weather Service Extreme Cold: use loose layers, stay dry and out of wind, carry a charged phone, tell others travel plans, and keep a winter vehicle kit.
  https://www.weather.gov/dlh/extremecold
- National Institute on Aging: older adults are at higher risk; check on people during cold weather, use warm loose layers, keep homes warm, and ask someone to check in during outages.
  https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/safety/cold-weather-safety-older-adults
